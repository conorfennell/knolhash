How the money works

H2H loser pays the winner €5. Lose to AVERAGE and your €5 goes into the AVERAGE pot, won by the next manager to beat AVERAGE.

The lowest two places of the week pay €5 each (ties share the places, e.g. €5 / €2.50 / €2.50) and the top scorer takes the €10 (split if tied).

Nobody goes below −€50 in a half (GW1–19, GW20–38): H2H is settled first, then the bottom two. Only gameweeks with confirmed bonus points count.
